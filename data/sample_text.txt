# Productivity Tips for Neurodivergent Individuals
- Use the "Pomodoro technique": Work for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break.
- Set up a distraction-free workspace.
- If switching tasks is difficult, use gentle reminders or visual timers.
- Break big tasks into small steps and celebrate small wins.

# Social Interaction Strategies
- If you don’t know what to say, start with: "Hi! How was your day?"
- If eye contact is uncomfortable, focus on the person’s forehead or nose.
- Use scripted responses for common conversations (e.g., ordering food, introductions).
- When overwhelmed, take deep breaths and find a quiet space.
